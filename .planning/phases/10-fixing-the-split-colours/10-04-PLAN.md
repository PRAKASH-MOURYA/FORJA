Renders a ListView of exercise rows, each showing:

**File 2 — lib/features/workout/finish_up_screen.dart:**
StatelessWidget pushed on the navigator stack (not a route — pushed as a page via `Navigator.push` from WorkoutScreen when user taps "Finish Workout").

Constructor: `const FinishUpScreen({required List<Exercise> incompleteExercises, required Function(String) onAddSets, required Function(String) onSkip, required VoidCallback onProceed})`.

`incompleteExercises`: exercises that have 0 completed sets and are not in skippedExerciseIds — computed by WorkoutScreen before pushing.

Shows a list: each row has exercise name, "Add Sets" TextButton (calls onAddSets(id) + Navigator.pop), "Skip" TextButton (calls onSkip(id)).

"Continue" button at bottom: enabled when incompleteExercises.isEmpty OR all rows have been acted on. Calls onProceed().

Show title "Finish Up" and subtitle "These exercises have no sets logged."
